# Asset credits

Every third-party asset in this folder must be listed here with source URL, author and license.
Only MIT, CC0 or CC-BY (with attribution) assets are allowed.

## Original art (this repository)

All tiles, characters, icons and the map draft are original pixel art generated by code in
`scripts/art/` (`npm run art`). They are covered by the repository's MIT license. No third-party
images are used. The style follows `docs/art/README.md` (palette, sizes and layout taken from the
reference video in `docs/reference/`).

|---|---|---|---|
| `tiles/village.png`, `tiles/village.json` | `scripts/art/tiles.ts`, `scripts/art/decor.ts` | Tiny Pixel People contributors | MIT |
| `characters/*.png`, `characters/index.json` | `scripts/art/characters.ts` | Tiny Pixel People contributors | MIT |
| `ui/icons.png`, `ui/icons.json`, `ui/glow.png` | `scripts/art/icons.ts` | Tiny Pixel People contributors | MIT |
